What we treat

Care organized around your mouth, not our org chart.

Six areas of practice, each staffed by someone who does mostly that — so you're never the first case of the week.

01

Preventive & Hygiene

Cleanings, exams, and sealants built around a schedule that fits your actual risk, not a default six months.

02

Restorative

Fillings, crowns, and same-day repairs using materials matched to your bite, not just what's fastest.

03

Cosmetic & Smile Design

Whitening, bonding, and veneers planned with digital previews, so you see the result before we start.

04

Orthodontics

Clear aligners and traditional braces for teens and adults, with progress checks every 6 weeks.

05

Oral Surgery

Extractions and implants performed in-house, with sedation options explained in plain language upfront.

06

Pediatric Dentistry

A calmer first visit for kids, with appointments paced to their comfort, not the clock.
